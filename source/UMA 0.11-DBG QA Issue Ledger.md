## Closure policy

Blocking and major reproduced defects are repaired automatically. Each repaired
row records first evidence, root cause, exact repair, focused retest, regression
retest, and final status. Non-reproduced categories close only with direct
current-build evidence; accepted G1/G2 evidence may establish a baseline but
does not substitute for DBG runtime or live proof where the Goal requires it.
